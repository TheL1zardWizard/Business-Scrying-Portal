Drivers

Spearman rank correlation between a metric and the star rating across every rated company of this type: +1.00 rises with the star in lockstep, 0 is unrelated, negative falls as stars rise. Tracking a star is not the same as causing it.

Typical value at each star

Seats filled is the median share of staff seats occupied. Open to applicants is the share of companies currently hiring. Daily pace compares each company's last day with its own weekly average; 100% is steady.

What Torn does not publish

  • Only your own company shows popularity, efficiency, environment, funds, trains, advertising budget, upgrades and value. For everyone else these are hidden, so they cannot be sampled.
  • Stars follow gross profit, and the highest star a company can hold depends on its lifetime gross revenue. Both come from the Torn wiki. The revenue cap is not public.
  • Popularity is your weekly income as a percentage of the best company of your type. It follows income and does not drive it. Efficiency is the average effectiveness of employees. Environment depends on staff room size and head count, and drug users and inactive employees lower it.
  • Advertising has a base effect of up to 40% that shrinks when more rivals of the same type advertise. Employee pay does not affect the star rating.
  • The My company tab combines your own hidden figures with the sampled lines, so you see both halves.

    How the lines moved

    What actually moved (selected type)

    Ratings change on Sundays. Between two snapshots that straddle a Sunday, every company that went up or down is recorded with its weekly income and customers from the earlier snapshot. These observed numbers replace guesswork about demotion lines, and My company uses them as they build up.

    How this was built

    • Every number comes from the official Torn API v2 company search, filtered by company type and star rating. No torn.com page was loaded or scraped, and no key is stored in this page.
    • Requests were paced at about one per second, well under the 100 per minute limit, using only the fields each company row needs.
    • For each type, every company with at least 1 star was collected. A first page of unrated (0 star) companies was added as a reference for the 1-star line.
    • Daily figures are each company's own last reported day, collected in the same pass. Spearman correlations, company age, seats filled, hiring status, income per customer and daily pace come from the same rows.
    • My company reads your own profile (company, detailed) with the key you save on this PC. The key stays in config.json and is never sent to this page. Staff names and ids are dropped before the page sees anything.
    • An earn line is the cut that best separates one star from the star below it (balanced accuracy). Signal quality is the AUC of that separation: sharp is 0.85 or more, moderate 0.70 or more, fuzzy below that.

    Read these limits first

    • This is one snapshot. Weekly income and customers are rolling figures, while ratings only change on Sundays, so some companies look out of place for a few days.
    • Demotion lines are inferred from where current holders sit. No actual star losses have been observed yet. A second snapshot after a Sunday update will show real movers.
    • Middle stars overlap heavily on income and customers. Age and other inputs Torn does not publish also matter, so treat middle lines as ranges.
    • Types with few companies at a star give weak lines. Those are marked Thin, and a line that falls below the star beneath it is marked Noisy.
